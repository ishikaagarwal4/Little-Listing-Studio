OPEN MODEL SETTINGS

Keep your ideas close.

Connect a local Ollama model for custom copy. The app sends your product details only to the Ollama server you choose. Without a connection, a built-in writer still works offline.

In Ollama, pull a model such as ollama run qwen2.5:3b, then leave Ollama running. For browser access, Ollama may need its CORS origin set to this local page.
OPEN INNOVATION, IN PRACTICE

Small shop. Your rules.

Most listing tools send every product note to a paid cloud API. Little Listing Studio can instead use an open-weight model running on your own computer through Ollama. That means you can choose or replace the model, keep drafts local, and avoid a fee for every listing.

The built-in writer also works without a model or internet connection, so you can try the workflow before setting anything up.
